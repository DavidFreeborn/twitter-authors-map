Each point represents a matched Twitter author in the corpus. A connection exists when one author retweets, quotes, or replies to another. Positions are computed using a force-directed layout algorithm that pulls connected authors together and pushes unconnected authors apart. Authors near each other share more direct or indirect connections. Point size reflects the author's total connection strength in the network.
Community labels describe prevalent post content, not every author or authors' identities. Provisional labels are shown as low confidence in the author panel.
