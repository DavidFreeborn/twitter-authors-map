Each point represents a Twitter user who posted about AI. A connection exists when one author retweets, quotes, or replies to another. Positions are computed using a force-directed layout algorithm that pulls connected authors together and pushes unconnected authors apart. Authors near each other share more direct or indirect connections. Point size reflects the author's total connection strength in the network.
