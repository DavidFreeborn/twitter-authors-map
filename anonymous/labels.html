Eight-topic comparison

The reviewed original-grid runner-up uses bigram TF-IDF, alpha 0.3, eta 0.01, seed 42 and 50 iterations. Its grid score is 0.7157, compared with 0.7109 for the twelve-topic model and 0.7231 for the automatic winner. Scores combine several diagnostics and do not establish a uniquely best model. The automatic winner was not retained because its full-population fit triggered concentration review. The reviewed eight-topic model had 61.2% matched leading-topic agreement in a controlled seed repeat, so these remain exploratory content descriptions.

Topic colour matching

The denominator is the eight-topic group’s author count; this is a directional measure, not intersection-over-union. The cutoff is applied before rounding. Other groups receive distinct colours.
